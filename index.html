Why this office

A small practice, on purpose

You will work with me directly. The person who answers the phone is the person who drafts your documents and the person who signs them.

Flat fees, quoted before you retain

You will know what the work costs before you commit to it. No hourly billing for planning documents or QDRO preparation, and no surprise invoices.

Planning that accounts for the money

I am an attorney and a CERTIFIED FINANCIAL PLANNER™. Retirement accounts, beneficiary designations and tax treatment get considered alongside the legal documents, not after.

Evenings and weekends
